# Phishing Awareness & Social Engineering Training Module

## Slide 1: Introduction to Phishing
* **Definition:** Phishing is a cyberattack mechanism where attackers masquerade as trusted entities to deceive individuals into revealing sensitive credentials, financial details, or corporate data.
* **Core Danger:** It exploits the human element rather than software vulnerabilities.

## Slide 2: Common Vectors of Phishing Attacks
* **Email Phishing:** Mass emails with malicious attachments or links mimicking legitimate brands.
* **Spear Phishing:** Highly targeted campaigns tailored specifically to an individual or enterprise.
* **Smishing & Vishing:** Attacks executed via SMS text messages or deceptive voice calls.
  
## Slide 3: Anatomy of a Phishing Email (Red Flags)
* **Urgent or Threatening Language:** "Your account will be suspended within 24 hours!"
* **Discrepancies in Domains:** Sender email address appears as `support@paypaI-security.com` instead of `paypal.com`[cite: 1].
* **Suspicious Hyperlinks:** Hovering over the link reveals an unrelated destination URL[cite: 1].

## Slide 4: Best Defensive Practices
1. **Verify Sender Identities:** Always validate corporate requests via secondary channels.
2. **Implement MFA:** Multi-Factor Authentication acts as a critical second line of defense.
3. **Inspect Hyperlinks:** Never click directly; manually type known domains into browser search bars[cite: 1].

   * A) Click the link immediately to secure the account.

   * C) Delete the email and verify directly with the internal IT department. *(Correct Answer: C)*
